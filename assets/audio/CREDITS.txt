Audio credits — Karks Cub Kingdom
=================================

All sound effects and music loops in this folder are by Kenney
(www.kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 means these files are free to use in personal, educational, and
commercial projects with no attribution required. This credit is included
anyway because Kenney's work makes projects like this possible.

Source packs (from Kenney's asset library):
  - Interface Sounds
  - Impact Sounds
  - Foley Sounds
  - Music Jingles
  - Music Loops

sfx/ — one-shot sound effects
  close_002.ogg             Interface Sounds  — laugh-meter drain tick
  confirmation_001.ogg      Interface Sounds  — typewriter/line-complete ding, boss transform cue
  confirmation_004.ogg      Interface Sounds  — laugh captured / review given pickup chime
  error_003.ogg             Interface Sounds  — early-punchline "groan" penalty
  impactGlass_heavy_001.ogg Impact Sounds     — HP bar "shatter"
  impactSoft_medium_002.ogg Impact Sounds     — soft thud (napkin/bottle hits)
  impactWood_heavy_002.ogg  Impact Sounds     — door/table slam boom
  jingles-hit_02.ogg        Music Jingles     — "ha" blip layered on the pickup chime
  jingles-hit_03.ogg        Music Jingles     — fake-death victory jingle (cut off by the scratch)
  jingles-hit_10.ogg        Music Jingles     — fanfare stinger
  minimize_006.ogg          Interface Sounds  — sad descending swoop (Epilogue A reaction)
  scratch_002.ogg           Interface Sounds  — DJ record scratch (fake-death gag)
  select_006.ogg            Interface Sounds  — Epilogue B phone-share ping
  stoneDrag2.ogg            Foley Sounds      — critic transformation rumble
  tick_001.ogg              Interface Sounds  — Epilogue B desk typing key blip
  woosh3.ogg                Foley Sounds      — dodge whoosh

music/ — per-beat score loops (renamed from Kenney's Music Loops pack; each
loops seamlessly)
  wacky-waiting.ogg         "Wacky Waiting"        — dinner (Beat 1)
  mission-plausible.ogg     "Mission Plausible"    — the critic boss fight
  time-driving.ogg          "Time Driving"         — chase beats (Aram, tech support)
  farm-frolics.ogg          "Farm Frolics"         — celebration / triumph stings
  sad-descent.ogg           "Sad Descent"          — bathroom epilogue / quiet sincere beat
  game-over.ogg             "Game Over"            — game over screen
  night-at-the-beach.ogg    "Night at the Beach"   — intro cinematic fallback (warm, nostalgic)

Not sample-replaced by design: the Karplus-Strong jaw-harp "twang" and the
speech-synthesis narration remain fully synthesized/native — see
game/index.html and intro/index.html.

The user's own uploaded song (theme.mp3, config slot) always overrides this
score when one is configured; Karks Cub Kingdom ships with its own song and
keeps it (USE_CUSTOM_SONG = true), with these loops wired in underneath as
the quiet cover during the download window and the eventual fallback score
for future games in the product line that don't configure a custom song.
