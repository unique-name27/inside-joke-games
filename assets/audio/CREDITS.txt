Audio credits — Karks Cub Kingdom
=================================

All sound effects and music loops in this folder are by Kenney
(www.kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 means these files are free to use in personal, educational, and
commercial projects with no attribution required. This credit is included
anyway because Kenney's work makes projects like this possible.

loops seamlessly). 22 of the pack's 24 tracks are used (19 from Loops/ +
3 from Retro/ — Retro Comedy and Retro Polka went unused, both under 8
seconds, too short/gimmicky to read as a bed loop rather than a stinger).

  wacky-waiting.ogg         "Wacky Waiting"        — default dinner (Beat 1) / the "upbeat" vibe's headline
  mission-plausible.ogg     "Mission Plausible"    — default boss (critic fight) / the "spy" vibe's headline

Not sample-replaced by design: the Karplus-Strong jaw-harp "twang" and the
speech-synthesis narration remain fully synthesized/native — see
game/index.html and intro/index.html.

The user's own uploaded song (theme.mp3, config slot) always overrides this
score when one is configured; Karks Cub Kingdom ships with its own song and
keeps it (USE_CUSTOM_SONG = true), with these loops wired in underneath as
the quiet cover during the download window and the eventual fallback score
for future games in the product line that don't configure a custom song.

Default music mix level (PHASE M): all music (the loops above and any
uploaded custom song alike) now plays at MUSIC_BASE_GAIN — clearly under
the one-shot SFX layer — see game/engine.js / intro/engine.js. Ducking
during dialogue/beats is unchanged in relative terms, just rescaled onto
this lower ceiling.
