FAQ

Questions people actually ask

What do you need from us?

A few real stories from your group, who's playing which role, and your group's catchphrase. About 10 minutes to fill out.

How long does it take?

About 48 hours from order to link, usually faster.

What if we don't have a "critic" / "boss" / etc.?

Every role except the host is optional — skip anything that doesn't fit your group. The game adjusts itself and still tells a complete, satisfying story either way.

Does it work on phones?

Yes — built mobile-first, landscape mode, touch controls included. Works on desktop too.

What if it doesn't work?

If we can't make your game work, you don't pay.

Who sees my stories?

Only used to build your game. Your link isn't listed or indexed anywhere public — nobody finds it by browsing, only by you sharing it.
