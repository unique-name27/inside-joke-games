FAQ

Questions people actually ask

What do you need from us?

Two or three ordinary stories your group actually retells, the phrase somebody always says, and which friends fit which roles. About 10 minutes.

Does this only work for one kind of group?

No. Roommates, coworkers, a team, a band, a group chat, a family, people who met on a trip — if your group has a running joke and a couple of stories, it works. The stories don't have to be funny on their own; the game does that part.

What does the permanent version add?

A permanent hosted address, your own uploaded song, and a human review before it ships. The free link never expires either way.

What if nobody fits a role?

Skip it. Every role except the main character is optional, and the game rewrites itself around whoever you cast — it still tells a complete story with two roles or with all six.

Does it work on phones?

Yes — built mobile-first, landscape mode, touch controls included. Works on desktop too.

What if it doesn't work?

If we can't make your game work, you don't pay.

Who sees my stories?

Only used to build your game. Your link isn't listed or indexed anywhere public — nobody finds it by browsing, only by you sharing it.
